DailyStox First-Time Setup

1. Right-click the DailyStox.zip file.
2. Click Extract All.
3. Open the extracted DailyStox folder.
4. Double-click install-node-local.bat one time.
5. Double-click start.bat to open DailyStox.

To stop DailyStox:
Double-click stop-dailystox.bat.

If something looks stuck:
1. Double-click stop-dailystox.bat.
2. Double-click start.bat.

You do not need GitHub.
You do not need to install Node.js manually.
